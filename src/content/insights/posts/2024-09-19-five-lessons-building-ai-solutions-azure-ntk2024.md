At the upcoming NT Conference 2024, our session **5 key lessons from building scalable and secure enterprise AI solutions with Azure**, on Tuesday, 24 September, will present the key challenges in developing AI solutions and integrating them into information systems.

We will share insights into the concrete challenges we face when implementing systems that enhance business processes with artificial intelligence. To demonstrate how we address these challenges, we will present **two AI systems**: a media coverage analysis system that **enriches our AI component with online sources**, and a document analysis system that can **securely process large volumes of company data**, enabling users to analyse information, compare texts in detail and generate documents.

<!--more-->

We will also present the technical architecture of our AI solutions, which are built on Azure AI services and **ensure data security as well as solution stability and scalability.** We will share our knowledge of how additional components can improve the reliability of AI solutions in terms of the accuracy and specificity of their responses, and equip AI components with a broad range of external information sources.

We will highlight the architecture used in KumuluzAI, which includes a wide range of components for working with artificial intelligence. These components enable **dynamic prompt enhancement** based on query context and the creation of context from large volumes of documentation. They also offer numerous data processing capabilities, such as retrieving and contextualising web data, intelligent classification and generating documentation in any format.

Biljana Prinčič, MSc, from Riko will offer her perspective on the organisational and business aspects of introducing an AI solution into a company, **sharing her first-hand practical experience** with a system built on the AI components we have developed.
