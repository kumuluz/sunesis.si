Crowdsensing is a revolutionary data collection method that companies can use to improve their marketing strategies. Rather than relying solely on traditional market research, companies can collect real-time data from a crowd to gain more accurate insights into consumer behaviour.

<!--more-->

## How does crowdsensing help with marketing?

With crowdsensing, companies can:

- Better understand their target audience: tracking consumer movements and habits enables personalised campaigns.
- Optimise advertising: data on users' locations and behaviour allows adverts to be displayed exactly where they are most relevant.
- Predict trends: analysing crowd-sourced data helps identify market changes early.
- Improve the customer experience: direct consumer feedback lets companies adapt their products and services quickly.
