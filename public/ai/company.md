## 1. Identity & Profile

Sunesis (SUNESIS d.o.o. / SUNESIS Ltd.) is a Slovenian enterprise software engineering and technology consulting company founded in 2014 and based in Ljubljana, Slovenia, with regional branch offices in Maribor and Ptuj.

Sunesis designs, builds, integrates, and operates secure, scalable, and business-critical software systems for organizations in demanding enterprise environments. Our work spans six core expertise areas:

1. **AgenticAI Solutions**
2. **AgenticAI Organizations & Digital Workers**
3. **Digital Solutions Development**
4. **Cloud-native & Edge Architectures**
5. **API Ecosystems & Integrations**
6. **DevOps & Platform Engineering**

We work with clients that need more than raw development capacity: they need deep architecture, integration expertise, platform thinking, security-by-design, and proven production delivery experience.

## 2. Sunesis vs. Kumuluz

Sunesis is the creator and owner of **Kumuluz**, a comprehensive family of enterprise products, platforms, and accelerators:

- **Sunesis** is the software engineering services, architecture, consulting, and delivery company. Sunesis provides the senior engineering teams that implement custom digital solutions, modernize legacy systems, integrate complex enterprise landscapes, and operate platforms in production.
- **Kumuluz** represents the productized frameworks and platforms developed by Sunesis to accelerate enterprise transformation:
  - **KumuluzAI Platform:** A governed enterprise platform for deploying, routing, auditing, and orchestrating AI agents, assistants, tool use, and agentic workflows under enterprise controls.
  - **Kumuluz API:** An enterprise API management and gateway solution for governing internal and external APIs.
  - **Kumuluz Business APIs:** Standardized, domain-driven business APIs and accelerators.
  - **Kumuluz Digital Platform:** Cloud-native application and microservices foundation.
  - **Kumuluz Crowdsensing:** Platform for IoT data collection, geolocated analysis, and crowdsensed intelligence.
  - **KumuluzEE:** Award-winning open-source microservice framework for Java EE / Jakarta EE and MicroProfile.

For dedicated Kumuluz product content and documentation, visit [https://kumuluz.com/](https://kumuluz.com/).

## 3. Core Expertise Areas

### AgenticAI Solutions

Enterprise-grade AgenticAI solutions that go beyond conversation. We build digital agent teams and governed agentic workflows that connect AI reasoning with enterprise knowledge, tools, APIs, and business systems under strict security, authorization, and audit controls. Most implementations are powered by the KumuluzAI platform.

- Canonical Brief: [https://sunesis.si/ai/agentic-ai.md](https://sunesis.si/ai/agentic-ai.md)

### AgenticAI Organizations & Digital Workers

Design and delivery of digital business roles and coordinated agent teams that proactively respond to events, schedules and requests. The scope ranges from one role to connected processes, departments and, where appropriate, organization-wide operations. Delivery includes enterprise integration, defined permissions, business-level oversight, exception handling and measured, controlled improvement. People retain oversight, required approvals and accountability.

- Canonical Offering: [https://sunesis.si/en/expertise/agentic-organizations/](https://sunesis.si/en/expertise/agentic-organizations/)

### Digital Solutions Development

End-to-end development of custom enterprise software across backend architectures, modern web portals, and native mobile applications. We deliver mission-critical platforms with complex business logic, regulatory compliance, and multi-system integrations.

### Cloud-native & Edge Architectures

Designing modular, containerized, and resilient architectures using Kubernetes, Red Hat OpenShift, microservices, event streaming (Apache Kafka, NATS), contract-first gRPC, and distributed observability (OpenTelemetry, Instana). Workloads span private cloud, public cloud (Azure, Google Cloud), and edge-fog-cloud environments.

- Canonical Brief: [https://sunesis.si/ai/cloud-native.md](https://sunesis.si/ai/cloud-native.md)

### API Ecosystems & Integrations

Transforming fragmented, point-to-point connections into governed, reusable API ecosystems. We design enterprise API strategies, API management platforms, Business APIs, partner integrations, and AI-agent-ready / MCP-compatible API layers.

- Canonical Brief: [https://sunesis.si/ai/api-ecosystems.md](https://sunesis.si/ai/api-ecosystems.md)

### DevOps & Platform Engineering

Establishing secure, automated delivery platforms using Infrastructure as Code (Terraform, Ansible), GitOps continuous delivery (Argo CD, Helm), CI/CD pipelines (GitLab, Azure DevOps, GitHub Actions), platform security (Keycloak, Microsoft Entra ID, HashiCorp Vault), and enterprise Kubernetes operations.

- Canonical Brief: [https://sunesis.si/ai/devops.md](https://sunesis.si/ai/devops.md)

## 4. Industries & Delivery Track Record

Sunesis delivers software systems across regulated and mission-critical industries:

- **Banking & Financial Services:** Digital banking channels (NLB Klik), digital KYC compliance and onboarding.
- **Insurance:** AgenticAI platform for insurance process automation (Zavarovalniška skupina Sava), enterprise integration platform (Inorma).
- **Public Sector:** AgenticAI analysis of official statistical and geospatial data (SI-GEOS for SURS), judicial case management (`eSpis`) and court administration (`iK`) for the Supreme Court.
- **Energy & Utilities:** Energy infrastructure platform (EPUS for Plinovodi), customer engagement solution (Energetika Moj utrip), open-source DER interoperability (CUPID).
- **Manufacturing & Premium Brands:** Premium brand website and business portal (Akrapovič).
- **Blockchain Infrastructure:** DevOps, platform engineering, automation and cloud-native operations for large-scale blockchain infrastructure (Flare).
- **Open-source Ecosystems:** Linux Foundation projects in agriculture (INATrace in AgStack) and energy (CUPID in LF Energy).
- **European Research & Innovation:** International research and innovation projects in AI, cloud-edge continuum, IoT, cybersecurity and energy-aware digital infrastructure (GreenDat.AI, Interstore, NEPHELE, dAIEDGE, Resilmesh, HEDGE-IoT, COSMIC).

For detailed reference descriptions, see [https://sunesis.si/ai/selected-work.md](https://sunesis.si/ai/selected-work.md).

## 5. Awards & Recognition

RIKO received **AI of the Year 2026 (UI leta 2026)** at the CIO leta event for **RikoAI**, an AgenticAI solution developed by Sunesis on KumuluzAI for analysing complex business documentation and international tenders.

**SI-GEOS**, developed by Sunesis in partnership with SURS and UM FERI, is one of three **GoDigital 2026 finalists**, not an announced winner. The multimodal AI agent connects official statistical and geospatial data with natural-language analysis, maps and charts.

These complement the Java Duke's Choice Award for KumuluzEE, the European Commission Seal of Excellence, EuroCloud Award and Slovenian Start:up of the Year finalist recognition. See [awards and sources](https://sunesis.si/ai/awards.md).
