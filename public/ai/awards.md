## 1. Awards and Recognitions

Sunesis and the Kumuluz product family have been recognized for innovation in cloud-native technologies, Java microservices, cloud services and enterprise digital platforms.
Solutions built by Sunesis have also been recognized for enterprise AgenticAI.

### AI of the Year 2026 (UI leta 2026) — RikoAI

- **Conferred at:** CIO leta, the Slovenian event for IT leaders (Brdo pri Kranju, 23 October 2026).
- **Recipient:** RIKO, for RikoAI — an AgenticAI solution built by Sunesis on the KumuluzAI platform.
- **Scope & Significance:** RikoAI is a multi-agent system that automates the analysis of complex business documentation and international tenders. The expert committee recognized the solution's innovation, technological maturity and measurable business impact. Source: [riko.si](https://www.riko.si/riko-prejel-prestizno-nagrado-ui-leta-2026/).

### GoDigital 2026 — Finalist (SI-GEOS AI agent)

- **Conferred by:** Association for Informatics and Telecommunications (ZIT) at the Chamber of Commerce and Industry of Slovenia (GZS) and SRIP GoDigital.
- **Recipient:** Sunesis, for the SI-GEOS AI agent, developed in partnership with the Statistical Office of the Republic of Slovenia (SURS) and the Faculty of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science of the University of Maribor (FERI).
- **Scope & Significance:** One of three finalists for the GoDigital 2026 award for the best digital project of the year; the winner is announced at the GoDigital conference on 8 December 2026. SI-GEOS is a multimodal AI agent that answers natural-language questions with official statistical and geospatial data, dynamic maps, charts and explanations. The project is co-funded by Eurostat, and the solution has been in use at SURS since September 2026. Source: [gzs.si](https://www.gzs.si/zdruzenje_za_informatiko_in_telekomunikacije/Novice/ArticleId/92066/znani-finalisti-za-nagrado-godigital-2026x).

### European Commission Seal of Excellence

- **Conferred by:** European Commission.
- **Recipient:** Sunesis, for its project proposal.
- **Scope & Significance:** A European quality label for proposals that meet the rigorous evaluation criteria of EU research and innovation programmes but cannot receive funding due to budget constraints. It recognises the quality and innovation potential of the proposal.

### Duke's Choice Award (2015)

- **Conferred by:** Oracle Corporation at JavaOne Conference (San Francisco, CA, USA).
- **Recipient:** KumuluzEE (developed by Sunesis).
- **Scope & Significance:** The Duke's Choice Award honors extreme innovation across the global Java community. KumuluzEE was recognized as one of the world's first lightweight microservice frameworks to bring modular, cloud-native microservice architecture to standard Java EE and Eclipse MicroProfile.

### EuroCloud Award

- **Recipient:** Sunesis, for the Kumuluz product family.
- **Scope & Significance:** Awarded for the most innovative cloud service. The expert jury recognized the innovation and competitive advantages of the Kumuluz integration platform.

### Slovenian Start:up of the Year — Finalist

- **Recipient:** Sunesis.
- **Scope & Significance:** Sunesis was selected as one of the finalists of the Slovenian Start:up of the Year competition, reflecting the early innovation potential, market relevance and technology ambition of Sunesis and its products.
