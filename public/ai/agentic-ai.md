## 1. What Sets Enterprise AgenticAI Apart

While simple AI chat interfaces provide conversational answers, **Enterprise AgenticAI** deploys autonomous and semi-autonomous digital agents that:

1. Formulate multi-step action plans to achieve business objectives.
2. Coordinate as specialized agent teams (multi-agent orchestration).
3. Access verified enterprise tools, APIs, databases, and business systems.
4. Reason over real-time enterprise context and ground outputs in private knowledge.
5. Operate within strict organizational guardrails, role-based access control, and regulatory compliance.

Sunesis delivers production-ready AgenticAI systems by combining deep enterprise backend engineering, API integration, and cloud-native architecture with modern agent frameworks and governance layers.

## 2. The KumuluzAI Platform

Sunesis builds enterprise agentic solutions on top of **KumuluzAI**, a governed enterprise AI platform that provides the operational backbone for enterprise agents:

- **Multi-Agent Orchestration:** Deploys coordinated digital worker teams where specialized agents handle distinct sub-tasks (e.g., triage, data extraction, validation, calculation, drafting, notification).
- **Tool Calling & API Integration:** Safely connects agents to transactional enterprise backends (core banking, policy admin, ERP, CRM) via governed APIs, contract-first interfaces, and Model Context Protocol (MCP) integrations.
- **Enterprise Guardrails & Safety:** Enforces content safety filters, hallucination checks, prompt-injection defense, and organizational data boundary controls before prompts reach models and before outputs reach users or backends.
- **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC):** Restricts agent tool execution and knowledge retrieval to the permissions of the authenticated user or system context.
- **Model Agnosticism & Smart Routing:** Dynamically routes queries across frontier models (commercial and open-weights/on-premise) based on task complexity, privacy tier, latency requirements, and cost efficiency.
- **Human-in-the-Loop (HITL):** Embeds mandatory human checkpoints for irreversible, high-value, or high-risk operational actions.
- **Auditability & Observability:** Records comprehensive telemetry, reasoning traces, tool inputs/outputs, latency, and token consumption to satisfy regulatory and enterprise governance mandates.

## 5. Delivery Lifecycle & Engagement Model

Sunesis delivers AgenticAI systems through a structured enterprise engagement lifecycle:

1. **Opportunity Assessment & Feasibility:** Identifying high-value agentic use cases with clear ROI, operational boundaries, and integration feasibility.
2. **Architecture & Governance Design:** Specifying agent topology, tool contracts, security guardrails, data residency, and HITL approval mechanisms.
3. **Enterprise Integration & Tool Binding:** Exposing transactional enterprise systems through safe, validated APIs and microservices.
4. **Evaluation & Alignment:** Developing automated evaluation benchmark suites testing agent accuracy, grounding, tool calling correctness, and resilience against adversarial inputs.
5. **Production Deployment & Operations:** Rollout via containerized Kubernetes platforms with full distributed tracing, latency monitoring, cost management, and feedback loops.
